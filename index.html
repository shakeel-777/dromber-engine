Frequently Asked Questions

How does Dromber maintain sub-1.2s response times on WhatsApp?

Through a multi-model cognitive pipeline. We deploy Claude 3.5 Sonnet for initial semantic entity extraction and deterministic tool calls, then stream final conversational formatting via Claude 3 Haiku. Coupled with Anthropic's Prompt Caching on catalog schemas, TTFT is reduced by over 70%.

How does the platform prevent hallucinated product prices?

Dromber enforces strict JSON tool calling against real-time commerce APIs (Amazon PA-API, Flipkart, Cuelinks). Recommendations are synthetically bound to live inventory responses; if an item is out of stock or price data fails verification, the engine automatically falls back to secondary catalog matches.

What are the requirements for connecting to WhatsApp Business API?

Dromber connects directly to any Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API app or BSP (Twilio, Gupshup, WATI). Simply point your webhook URL to our ingestion endpoint and configure your verify token in our dashboard.

How does Dromber handle regulatory compliance and tax attribution?

Every generated deep link passes through our attribution compiler, automatically appending required ASCI disclosure hashtags (#ad, #affiliate) and recording Section 194H TDS (2% or 5%) and GST identifiers in the conversion webhook telemetry.
