🔒 Privacy & Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At Dromber Technologies (https://dromber.site), we prioritize the confidentiality and protection of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent protocols for collecting, storing, and utilizing customer data when you browse our catalog or purchase our digital educational blueprints and AI prompt packs.

By utilizing our platform or initiating a transaction, you consent to the data practices described herein, compliant with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 of India.

1 Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization and only collect information essential for fulfilling your developer requests:

  • Developer Contact Data: Your name, organization, and work email address provided during sandbox provisioning to dispatch API keys, webhook endpoints, and integration credentials.
  • Customer Support Records: Technical queries submitted via WhatsApp or email, used exclusively to troubleshoot API integration and assist with setup.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymous telemetry (such as IP-derived latency data, model token throughput, and request headers) used solely for rate limiting, latency SLA compliance, and cluster health monitoring.

🛡️ Zero Payment Data Retention: Dromber Technologies NEVER stores, processes, or retains credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs. All payment processing is encrypted and handled directly by Razorpay under RBI PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.

2 How We Utilize Your Information

We utilize developer data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To dispatch API keys and webhook simulator access for the Developer Sandbox (Free), Production Commerce Engine (₹3,999/mo), and Enterprise Cluster immediately upon registration.
  • To dispatch critical latency SLA notifications, security advisories, and model gateway updates.
  • To send transaction confirmations and Razorpay invoice receipts for production licenses.
  • To enforce API rate limits and defend against automated abuse.

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade developer contact details to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3 Authorized Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver an instant and secure API experience, we interface with trusted enterprise service providers:

Razorpay Software Ltd.

Payment gateway processing UPI, cards, and net banking under RBI regulatory guidelines.

EmailJS

Automated transactional email dispatch delivering API tokens and integration credentials.

Netlify Inc.

High-speed global Edge CDN and serverless function runtime hosting the static storefront.

OpenRouter (AI Routing)

Zero-cost AI routing for Seron Concierge. No user contact data or payment info is ever passed to the LLM.

4 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Dromber Technologies operates a lean, privacy-first interface. We do not use cross-site tracking cookies, Facebook Pixel, or invasive behavioral tracking.

We only employ minimal in-memory local state to maintain your active shopping session, facilitate the checkout modal, and prevent abuse of the Seron AI chat widget.

5 Data Retention & User Rights

We retain transactional contact logs (customer name, email, and Razorpay payment ID) for standard accounting and tax compliance under Indian financial reporting requirements.

You have the right to request access to your stored records or request complete deletion of non-essential records by messaging our grievance officer directly on WhatsApp with your payment reference.
